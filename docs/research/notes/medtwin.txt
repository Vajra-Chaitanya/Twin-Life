===============================================================================
13. HEALTH TWIN
===============================================================================

13.1 Introduction

The Health Twin is a digital representation of an individual's health profile.
It continuously monitors the user's medical information, predicts disease
risks, recommends preventive measures, and communicates with other Twins
through the Orchestrator.

The Health Twin updates itself whenever new health information such as blood
reports, lifestyle changes, medications, or wearable data becomes available.

Instead of storing only historical records, the Health Twin continuously
simulates future health conditions and predicts possible risks.

===============================================================================
13.2 Responsibilities
===============================================================================

The Health Twin performs the following tasks:

• Create a digital representation of the user's health.

• Analyze uploaded medical reports.

• Predict disease risks.

• Recommend preventive actions.

• Suggest doctor consultation.

• Schedule medicine reminders.

• Schedule doctor appointments.

• Estimate future medical expenses.

• Communicate with Finance Twin.

• Communicate with Insurance Twin.

===============================================================================
13.6 Prediction Modules
===============================================================================

The Health Twin consists of multiple independent prediction modules.

Health Twin

│

├── Diabetes Prediction Module

├── Heart Disease Prediction Module

├── Obesity Prediction Module

├── Hypertension Prediction Module

├── Lifestyle Analysis Module

├── Health Score Module

└── Future Risk Prediction Module

Each module predicts a specific aspect of the user's health.
