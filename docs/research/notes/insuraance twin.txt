===============================================================================
15. INSURANCE TWIN
===============================================================================

15.1 Introduction

The Insurance Twin is a digital representation of an individual's health
insurance profile. It continuously monitors insurance policies, coverage,
claim limits, premium payments, and eligibility conditions to estimate
medical coverage and out-of-pocket expenses.

Unlike traditional insurance applications that simply store policy details,
the Insurance Twin actively collaborates with the Health Twin and Finance
Twin to optimize healthcare affordability and reduce financial burden.

===============================================================================
15.2 Responsibilities
===============================================================================

The Insurance Twin performs the following tasks:

• Store insurance policy information.

• Analyze insurance coverage.

• Estimate claim eligibility.

• Calculate out-of-pocket medical expenses.

• Compare treatment cost with insurance coverage.

• Monitor policy expiry.

• Send premium renewal reminders.

• Suggest better insurance plans.

• Communicate with Health Twin.

• Communicate with Finance Twin.
