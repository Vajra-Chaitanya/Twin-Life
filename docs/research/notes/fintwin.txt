===============================================================================
14. FINANCE TWIN
===============================================================================

14.1 Introduction

The Finance Twin is a digital representation of an individual's financial
status. It continuously monitors income, expenses, savings, loans,
investments, and financial commitments to estimate the user's financial
health and ability to support future healthcare and life decisions.

Unlike traditional expense tracking applications, the Finance Twin does not
only record transactions. It predicts future financial risks, evaluates
affordability, estimates healthcare expenditure, and collaborates with the
Health Twin to generate optimized life recommendations.

===============================================================================
14.2 Responsibilities
===============================================================================

The Finance Twin performs the following tasks:

• Create a digital representation of the user's financial profile.

• Analyze monthly income and expenses.

• Monitor savings and investments.

• Track loans and EMI payments.

• Predict financial health.

• Estimate future savings.

• Calculate emergency fund status.

• Evaluate affordability of healthcare recommendations.

• Estimate future medical expenditure.

• Communicate with Health Twin.

• Communicate with Insurance Twin.
