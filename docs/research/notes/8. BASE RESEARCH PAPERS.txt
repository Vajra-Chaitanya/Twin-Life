The proposed system is developed by extending the concepts presented in
existing Digital Twin research.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Paper 1

Title:

Patient-Specific Digital Twins for Personalized Healthcare:
A Hybrid AI and Simulation-Based Framework

Publisher:

IEEE Access (2025)

Purpose:

This paper introduces a Healthcare Digital Twin capable of creating a
personalized virtual patient using AI and simulation techniques.

Features:

• Patient Digital Twin
• Disease Prediction
• Continuous Monitoring
• Treatment Planning
• Simulation
• Explainable AI

Research Gap:

• Healthcare only
• No Financial Integration
• No Insurance
• No Multi-Agent System
• No Orchestrator
• No Scenario Negotiation

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Paper 2

Title:

Digital Twin: Financial Technology's Next Frontier of Robo-Advisors

Introduces Financial Digital Twins capable of modeling customer finances and
providing personalized financial planning.

Features:

• Financial Modeling
• Financial Simulation
• Robo Advisory
• Personalized Recommendations

Research Gap:

• No Medical Cost Prediction

11. COMPLETE SYSTEM WORKFLOW
===============================================================================

TwinLife AI follows an event-driven, orchestrator-based workflow where every
change in user information automatically triggers intelligent agents to analyze,
predict, simulate, and recommend personalized actions.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 : USER REGISTRATION

The user creates an account by entering:

• Name
• Email
• Password

After authentication, the user is redirected to the onboarding process.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 2 : DIGITAL TWIN CREATION

The application creates an empty Digital Twin consisting of:

• Health Twin
• Finance Twin
• Insurance Twin

Each Twin stores information related to its domain.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 3 : USER PROFILE SETUP

The application asks the user to complete three sections.

1. Health Information

2. Financial Information

3. Insurance Information

Once completed,

↓

The Digital Twin is initialized.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 4 : HEALTH ANALYSIS

The Health Twin sends user data to trained Machine Learning models.

↓

The models predict

• Diabetes Risk
• Heart Disease Risk
• Hypertension Risk
• Obesity Risk

↓

The results are stored inside the Health Twin.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 5 : FINANCIAL ANALYSIS

The Finance Twin analyzes

• Income
• Expenses
• Savings
• Loans
• EMI

↓

Financial models estimate

• Financial Health Score
• Debt Risk
• Savings Forecast
• Emergency Fund Status

↓

Results are stored inside Finance Twin.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 6 : INSURANCE ANALYSIS

The Insurance Twin reads

• Coverage
• Premium
• Claim Limit
• Diseases Covered

↓

Calculates

Estimated Out-of-pocket Expenses

↓

Coverage Adequacy

↓

Claim Eligibility

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 7 : ORCHESTRATOR ACTIVATION

After all Twins are updated,

the Orchestrator becomes active.

Responsibilities:

• Detect data changes

• Trigger required AI agents

• Coordinate communication

• Resolve conflicts

• Start simulations

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 8 : AGENT COLLABORATION

The Orchestrator activates

↓

Health Agent

↓

Finance Agent

↓

Insurance Agent

↓

Risk Agent

↓

Negotiation Agent

All agents exchange information before making decisions.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 9 : SCENARIO SIMULATION

The user can simulate situations such as

"What if I start walking every day?"

"What if I buy a bike?"

"What if I miss EMI?"

"What if I lose my job?"

"What if my diabetes progresses?"

The Simulation Engine predicts future outcomes.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 10 : RECOMMENDATION GENERATION

The system combines

Health Prediction

+

Finance Prediction

+

Insurance Analysis

↓

Optimized Recommendation

Example

Treatment Recommended

↓

Financial Affordability

↓

Insurance Coverage

↓

Final Treatment Plan

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 11 : PROACTIVE MONITORING

Unlike ordinary applications,

TwinLife AI continuously monitors

• Health

• Finance

• Insurance

↓

Automatically sends

Medicine Reminders

EMI Alerts

Insurance Renewal Alerts

Doctor Appointment Reminders

Health Risk Alerts

Financial Risk Alerts

without requiring user interaction.
